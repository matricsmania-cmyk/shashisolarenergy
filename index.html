Solar Energy Generation: How It Works

The process of solar energy generation is simple yet highly effective.

Here’s how a solar system works:

  • Solar panels absorb sunlight.
  • Panels convert sunlight into direct current (DC).
  • A solar inverter converts DC into alternating current (AC).
  • Electricity powers household or commercial appliances.
  • Extra power is exported to the grid through net metering.
