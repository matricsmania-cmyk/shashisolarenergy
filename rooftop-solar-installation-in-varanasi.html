Types of Rooftop Solar Systems

Before choosing a rooftop solar system, it is important to understand different types available in the market.

On-Grid Solar System

An On Grid Solar System in Varanasi is connected to the electricity grid. It helps reduce electricity bills and supports net metering.

Best for:

  • Homes
  • Offices
  • Shops
  • Schools
  • Commercial buildings

Off-Grid Solar System

Off-grid systems use batteries to store electricity and provide backup during power cuts.

Best for:

  • Rural areas
  • Locations with frequent electricity outages

Hybrid Solar System

Hybrid solar combines battery backup with grid connectivity. It offers both energy savings and power backup.

Best for:

  • Homes needing uninterrupted power supply
  • Businesses with critical electricity requirements
