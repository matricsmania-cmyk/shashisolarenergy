Types of Solar Systems Available in Varanasi

On-Grid Solar System

An on-grid solar system is connected directly to the electricity grid. Excess electricity generated by the system can be exported through net metering.

Benefits include:

  • Lower installation cost
  • No battery requirement
  • Maximum savings
  • Easy maintenance

Off-Grid Solar System

Off-grid systems store electricity in batteries and operate independently from the grid.

Hybrid Solar System

Hybrid systems combine grid connectivity with battery backup for uninterrupted power supply.
