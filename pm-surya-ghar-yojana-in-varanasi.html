What is PM Surya Ghar Yojana and How Does It Work in Varanasi?

PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana in Varanasi is a government initiative designed to encourage residential rooftop solar adoption across India. The scheme provides financial support to homeowners who install eligible rooftop solar systems.

The scheme works through the official National Portal for Rooftop Solar, where applicants can register online, apply for solar subsidy, select approved vendors, track application status, upload required documents, and receive subsidy benefits.

How Net Metering Works Under PM Surya Ghar Yojana in Varanasi?

Net metering is one of the most valuable benefits available under PM Surya Ghar Yojana in Varanasi. It allows homeowners to export excess solar electricity to the utility grid and receive credits against electricity bills.

Suppose your rooftop solar system generates 12 units per day, but your home consumes 8 units per day. The remaining 4 units can be exported to the electricity grid through net metering. These exported units are adjusted against future electricity consumption, helping further reduce monthly expenses.

For most homes with reliable grid access, on grid solar in Varanasi is the most common choice because it works without batteries and uses net metering to improve savings.
